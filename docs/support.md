# Dock Finder Support

Dock Finder helps Citi Bike riders in New York City find an open dock before they get there, hands-free. It's in testing on TestFlight.

**Contact:** dbh2054@stern.nyu.edu. Testers can also send feedback from the TestFlight app: take a screenshot in Dock Finder, or open Dock Finder in TestFlight and tap **Send Beta Feedback**.

## Getting started

1. Install Dock Finder from your TestFlight invite. It needs iOS 26.5 or later.
2. When asked, allow location **Always**. Dock Finder needs it to tell you where to dock with your phone locked in your pocket.
3. Allow **Motion & Fitness**, so arrival alerts only go off when you're biking.
4. Save the places you ride to, like Home, Work or School, and pick a usual dock for each.

## Things to try

Say these to Siri:

- "Ask Dock Finder to start a ride to School"
- "Ask Dock Finder to find a dock near me"
- "Ask Dock Finder to check my Home dock"
- "Ask Dock Finder to direct me there", for spoken turn-by-turn directions to the dock
- "Ask Dock Finder to stop directions"
- "Ask Dock Finder to end my ride"
- "Ask Dock Finder how many bikes are out"

You can also ask from the home screen in the app.

## Common problems

**Siri runs the wrong action or doesn't recognize the phrase.** Start with "Ask Dock Finder". If it still misbehaves after an update, open the app once, then try again. iOS refreshes its list of phrases when the app opens.

**No alert when I ride up to a saved place.** Check that location is set to **Always** and Motion & Fitness is on (Settings → Dock Finder), and that arrival alerts are on for that place.

**Directions stopped talking.** Make sure the ringer volume is up and Dock Finder still has location access. Directions also stop when you arrive at the dock.

**Dock counts look wrong.** Dock Finder uses Citi Bike's live public feed, which can lag a minute or two behind the station.
